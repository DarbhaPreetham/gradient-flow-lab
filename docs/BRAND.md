# ChromoWeave Brand System

## Direction: Woven Prism

ChromoWeave’s identity comes from the game itself: colored tiles crossing and settling into a complete spectrum. The brand should feel luminous, tactile, intelligent, and playful without becoming noisy or childish.

## Logo

The primary symbol is a compact woven 3×3 tile grid. It must remain recognizable at app-icon and favicon sizes. Use the symbol without the name in small spaces; use the symbol with the ChromoWeave name for store, press, and promotional material.

Keep clear space equal to one outer tile around the logo. Do not recolor individual tiles, distort the grid, add extra glow, or place it over a busy gradient.

## Typography

- **Display:** Syne 700–800 for the ChromoWeave name and important celebrations.
- **Interface:** Space Grotesk 400–700 for controls, progress, level names, and longer copy.
- Letter spacing is neutral for normal text. Small uppercase labels may use generous positive spacing.

## Core palette

- **Ink:** near-black violet for the primary dark canvas.
- **Paper:** cool white for high-contrast text and the light theme.
- **Iris:** the primary action color.
- **Ultraviolet:** the signature ChromoWeave accent.
- **Magenta:** celebration and collection accent.
- **Cyan:** progress and guidance accent.

The game boards own the full spectrum. Navigation and controls use the core palette sparingly so each tapestry remains the visual focus.

## Motion and sound

Motion should feel like a tile sliding into a loom: short travel, restrained settling, and no constant shimmer. Background texture stays still. Reduced-motion preferences remove decorative transitions.

Sound remains generative and pentatonic. Button and tile sounds should be brief, always harmonious, and quieter than the completion moment. There is no continuous background music.

## Voice

Warm, concise, and quietly encouraging. Prefer “weave,” “tapestry,” “spectrum,” and “flow.” Avoid urgency, streak pressure, failure language, and generic wellness claims.
